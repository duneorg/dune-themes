  Eventually adapted from HTML5 UP for Dune — sparse #header and #signup-form over

  in this port.
---

Upstream [Eventually](https://html5up.net/eventually) is a coming-soon shell: `#header`
with a site title and optional tagline, a centered `#signup-form`, `#footer` icons plus
copyright, and a client-side `#bg` rotator cycling background images. This Dune port keeps
that sparse first viewport — Blog, Search, Archives, and About still exist as real routes
for the thin demo posts, even though the landing itself stays deliberately minimal.
