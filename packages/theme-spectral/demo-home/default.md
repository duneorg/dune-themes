This paragraph renders inside `#one`, the first content section below
Spectral's tall `#banner` — the block that opens with "Built for Dune CMS."
Spectral's schema is intentionally thin: only `show_html5up_credit` and
`footer_text` exist, because upstream Spectral has no per-page copy to
seed beyond the design itself. There's no tagline or hero title knob here
— the banner heading always mirrors the page title, and the "Get Started"
button under it always points at `/blog`.

Scroll past this section and the icon row (Blog / Search / Archives) to
reach `#two`, a three-panel spotlight band pairing an image with a heading
and button for each — Blog, Search, and Archives again, restated with more
visual weight. `#three` follows with a four-item feature grid (Blog,
About, Search, Archives), and `#cta` closes the page with a stacked
two-button call to action before the plain-text footer with its icon row
and copyright line.
