A faithful-adjacent port of [HTML5 UP Paradigm Shift](https://html5up.net/paradigm-shift)
for Dune. Home opens on the `banner_title` / `tagline` intro with a single
arrow-scroll cue into `#first`, then stacks six sections — an explore panel
with icon links to Blog/Search/Archives/About, a four-image gallery, a
get-started CTA, and a closing "get in touch" section with the CC BY credit
and Dune/HTML5 UP icon links.

Inner pages drop the section stack for a single content block, but keep the
same `dune-nav` button row and credit line, so the CC BY attribution never
disappears once you leave `/`.
