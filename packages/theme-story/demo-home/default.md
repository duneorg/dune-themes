A faithful-adjacent port of [HTML5 UP Story](https://html5up.net/story) for
Dune. The home route keeps the full upstream sequence: fullscreen banner,
alternating-orientation spotlights, a six-image lightbox gallery, and a closing
CTA row — all before the icon-footer with its Blog/Search/Archives links.

Inner pages (About, Search, Archives, individual posts) drop the spotlights but
keep the same divided-wrapper banner and icon footer, so the chrome never feels
like it belongs to a different site once you leave `/`.
