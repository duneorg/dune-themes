Upstream [Verti](https://html5up.net/verti) is a business landing:
`#header-wrapper` with logo + tagline + nav, a boxed `#banner` with dual CTAs,
three `#features-wrapper` image cards, then sidebar thumbnails beside a content
column, closing on a three-widget `#footer`. This Dune port keeps that chrome
and points every feature card, thumbnail, and button at Blog, Search, Archives,
or About.
