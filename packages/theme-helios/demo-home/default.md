A faithful-adjacent port of [HTML5 UP Helios](https://html5up.net/helios) for
Dune. The header carries the site title, a `tagline`-driven subline, and a
"Get Started" scroll button; the home route adds the `banner_title` headline
above a five-card carousel reel (Blog, Search, Archives, About, Welcome) before
settling into the same three-column feature strip every inner page shares.

Compare the reel spacing and banner headline against
[html5up.net/helios](https://html5up.net/helios) — that comparison is the
whole point of seeding `tagline` and `banner_title` on this demo instead of
leaving them empty.
