Upstream [Miniport](https://html5up.net/miniport) is built around one pairing: a portrait
next to a headline, then scrolly-anchored sections underneath. This Dune port keeps that
rhythm on `/` and `/home` — `home_headline` sets the line beside the portrait, `hero_image`
swaps the portrait itself — before Blog, About, Search, and Archives take over as real
routes below the fold.
